Chia-Yuan Wu

About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISE) at Lehigh University under the supervision of Prof. Daniel P. Robinson. I received my M.S. in Industrial Engineering and Management in 2017 and my B.B.A. in Management Science in 2015, both from National Chiao Tung University in Hsinchu, Taiwan.

My research focuses on bilevel optimization and fairness in collaborative machine learning, with an emphasis on mitigating unfairness through synthetic data generation, preserving privacy, and improving robustness against unreliable clients.

Prior to joining Lehigh University, I worked as a system design engineer at Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and AU Optronics (AUO), both based in Hsinchu, Taiwan, where I developed data-driven systems that strengthened supply-chain decision making, including automated planning tools, optimization pipelines, and real-time dashboards for large-scale manufacturing operations. I am currently completing a co-op in Digital Supply Chain at Skyworks Solutions, where I contribute to supply chain planning and process improvement initiatives.
